## 🚀 Installation Guide

### Installing the Enhanced APK

1. **Download the APK**
   - Download the `Mr.Comic-Enhanced.apk` file to your Android device
   - File size: Approximately 55MB

2. **Enable Unknown Sources** (if needed)
   - Go to **Settings** > **Security** > **Unknown Sources**
   - Enable installation from unknown sources
   - *Note: This step may vary depending on your Android version*

3. **Install the APK**
   - Locate the downloaded APK file using a file manager
   - Tap on the APK file to begin installation
   - Follow the on-screen prompts
   - Grant any requested permissions

4. **Launch the App**
   - Find the Mr.Comic icon on your home screen or app drawer
   - Tap to launch and enjoy the new video splash screen!

### Updating from Previous Version

If you have an older version of Mr.Comic installed:

1. **Backup Your Data** (recommended)
   - Your comic library and reading progress should be preserved
   - Consider backing up important data as a precaution

2. **Install Over Existing Version**
   - The new version will update your existing installation
   - All your comics and settings will be preserved

## 🎬 Video Splash Screen

### What You'll See

When you launch Mr.Comic, you'll be greeted with a beautiful video splash screen that showcases the app's branding and creates an engaging start to your comic reading experience.

### How It Works

#### On Android 12 and Later
- Uses the modern Android SplashScreen API
- Displays a branded splash screen with smooth animations
- Automatically transitions to the main app

#### On Android 7-11
- Shows a custom video splash screen
- Features high-quality video content
- Includes smooth transitions and animations

### Interacting with the Splash Screen

**Skip the Splash Screen:**
- **Tap anywhere** on the screen to skip immediately
- The app will transition to the main interface right away

**Automatic Progression:**
- If you don't tap, the splash will automatically finish after a few seconds
- The timing is optimized for the best user experience

### Splash Screen Behavior

**First Launch:**
- The splash screen may take slightly longer as the app initializes
- Subsequent launches will be faster

**Low Memory Devices:**
- The app automatically optimizes the splash screen for devices with limited memory
- You may see a simplified version that still looks great

**No Internet Required:**
- The splash screen works completely offline
- All video content is embedded in the app

## 🎨 Dynamic App Icons

### Available Icon Designs

Choose from 4 beautiful icon designs:

1. **Classic** - The traditional Mr.Comic icon
2. **Modern** - A sleek, contemporary design
3. **Colorful** - Vibrant and eye-catching
4. **Minimalist** - Clean and simple

### How to Change Your App Icon

1. **Open Mr.Comic**
   - Launch the app from your current icon

2. **Go to Settings**
   - Tap the **Settings** button (usually in the main menu)
   - Look for the gear icon ⚙️

3. **Find App Icon Settings**
   - Scroll down to find **"App Icon"** or **"Change App Icon"**
   - Tap on this option

4. **Select Your Icon**
   - You'll see a grid showing all 4 available icons
   - The currently selected icon will be highlighted
   - Tap on any icon to select it

5. **Apply the Change**
   - Tap **"Apply"** or **"Save"** to confirm your choice
   - The change will take effect immediately

### What Happens When You Change Icons

**Immediate Update:**
- Your home screen icon changes right away
- The app drawer icon also updates
- No need to restart your device

**Launcher Compatibility:**
- Works with all major Android launchers
- Some custom launchers may require a restart
- The change is permanent until you change it again

**Multiple Icons:**
- You won't see duplicate icons on your home screen
- Only the selected icon will be visible
- Previous icons are automatically hidden

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Splash Screen Issues

**Problem: Splash screen appears black or doesn't show**
- **Solution**: Restart the app
- **Cause**: Usually a temporary loading issue
- **Prevention**: Ensure you have enough free storage space

**Problem: Video doesn't play smoothly**
- **Solution**: Close other apps to free up memory
- **Alternative**: The app will automatically use a simpler splash screen
- **Note**: This is normal on older devices

**Problem: Splash screen takes too long**
- **Solution**: Tap anywhere on the screen to skip
- **Note**: First launch may take longer than subsequent launches

### App Icon Issues

**Problem: Icon doesn't change after selection**
- **Solution 1**: Restart your launcher app
  - Go to **Settings** > **Apps** > **[Your Launcher]** > **Force Stop**
  - Then reopen your launcher
- **Solution 2**: Restart your device
- **Solution 3**: Try changing to a different icon first, then back to your preferred one

**Problem: Multiple icons appear on home screen**
- **Solution**: This is rare but can happen on some custom launchers
- **Fix**: Remove the duplicate icons manually
- **Prevention**: Use the built-in Android launcher or popular launchers like Nova

**Problem: Icon change option not visible**
- **Check**: Make sure you're using the enhanced version of the app
- **Update**: Ensure you have the latest version installed
- **Restart**: Try restarting the app

### General Issues

**Problem: App crashes on startup**
- **Solution 1**: Restart your device
- **Solution 2**: Clear the app's cache
  - Go to **Settings** > **Apps** > **Mr.Comic** > **Storage** > **Clear Cache**
- **Solution 3**: Reinstall the app (your data should be preserved)

**Problem: App runs slowly**
- **Check**: Available storage space (need at least 100MB free)
- **Close**: Other running apps to free up memory
- **Restart**: Your device if the problem persists

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### About the Enhanced Features

**Q: Do the new features affect my existing comics and reading progress?**
A: No, all your existing comics, bookmarks, and reading progress are completely preserved. The new features are additions that don't interfere with your existing data.

**Q: Can I disable the video splash screen?**
A: While there's no setting to completely disable it, you can always tap to skip it immediately. The splash screen is designed to be quick and non-intrusive.

**Q: How often can I change my app icon?**
A: You can change your app icon as often as you like. There are no restrictions or cooldown periods.

**Q: Will changing the app icon affect my shortcuts or widgets?**
A: App shortcuts and widgets will continue to work normally. They may update to reflect the new icon design.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Does the video splash screen use internet data?**
A: No, the video splash screen is completely offline. All video content is embedded in the app and doesn't require an internet connection.

**Q: Why is the APK file larger than before?**
A: The enhanced version includes video assets and additional icon resources, which increase the file size by approximately 10-15MB. This is a one-time download cost for significantly enhanced features.

**Q: Will these features drain my battery faster?**
A: The impact on battery life is minimal (less than 1-2% additional usage). The video splash screen only plays briefly when you launch the app, and the icon system has no ongoing battery impact.

**Q: Do these features work on tablets?**
A: Yes! Both the video splash screen and dynamic app icons work perfectly on tablets and adapt to different screen sizes automatically.

### Compatibility Questions

**Q: My device is running Android 6.0. Will this work?**
A: Unfortunately, this enhanced version requires Android 7.0 or higher. You can continue using the standard version of Mr.Comic on Android 6.0.

**Q: I'm using a custom launcher. Will the icon changes work?**
A: The icon changes work with most launchers, including popular ones like Nova Launcher, Action Launcher, and Microsoft Launcher. Some highly customized launchers may require a restart to show the new icon.

**Q: Does this work on Android TV or Chromebooks?**
A: The app is designed for phones and tablets. While it may work on Android TV or Chromebooks, the experience isn't optimized for these platforms.

### Troubleshooting Questions

**Q: The app seems slower than before. What should I do?**
A: Try these steps in order:
1. Restart the app
2. Clear the app's cache (Settings > Apps > Mr.Comic > Storage > Clear Cache)
3. Restart your device
4. Ensure you have at least 200MB of free storage space

**Q: I don't see the new features. Am I using the right version?**
A: Check that you've downloaded and installed the enhanced APK. You should see a video splash screen when launching the app, and there should be an "App Icon" option in the settings menu.

**Q: Can I go back to the old version?**
A: Yes, you can uninstall the enhanced version and reinstall the standard version. However, you'll lose access to the new features. Your comic data will be preserved.
